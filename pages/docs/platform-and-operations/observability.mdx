# Observability

> See what your functions did, find what went wrong, and spot trends across runs.

Inngest records every event, run, and step without extra instrumentation. Pick the view that matches your question:

- [Inspect events and runs](/docs/platform-and-operations/inspect-events-and-runs): trace a failed run back to its event and step.
- [Traces](/docs/platform-and-operations/traces): follow each step, wait, and retry in a single run.
- [Metrics](/docs/platform-and-operations/metrics): spot changes in volume, failures, and backlog across a function or app.
- [Insights](/docs/platform-and-operations/insights): query historical events, runs, and steps with SQL.
