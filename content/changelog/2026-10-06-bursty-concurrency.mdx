export const title = "Bursty Concurrency: spikes run up to 3x your limit";
export const date = "2026-10-06";

We've added bursty concurrency for Pro and above accounts. When steps need more concurrency than your account limit, Inngest raises that limit to 3x for up to 60 minutes a month.

![Three bursts raise a concurrency limit of 100 toward a ceiling of 300 and use up the monthly hour](/assets/blog/new-bursty-concurrency-coverage-for-spikes/bursty-concurrency.gif)

- **Size** — Each burst starts at 3x your account concurrency limit.
- **Counting** — The budget is 60 minutes. Each minute in which steps run above your limit spends one minute, however many steps start.
- **Refill** — The budget resets every month, at a time unique to your account.
- **Notifications** — Admins receive an email at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of the budget.
- **Billing** — Bursts are not charged. Add concurrency on your [billing page](https://app.inngest.com/billing) if you use the full hour.

[Function-level concurrency](/docs/durable-execution/flow-control/concurrency?ref=changelog-bursty-concurrency#limit-one-function) you set overrides a burst, so a function with its own limit does not burst.
